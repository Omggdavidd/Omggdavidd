## Hey, I'm David 👋

I like building software around messy, real-world problems—especially when the job is turning unreliable inputs into something people can actually trust and use.

Right now, I'm a software developer at **Adknown**, co-founder of **Cribb**, and a final-year **Computing (Co-op)** student at the University of Guelph. Most of my work sits somewhere between full-stack product engineering, data, and AI. I care about shipping things, measuring whether they work, and making them understandable to the next person who touches the code.

## Experience in a nutshell

- At **Adknown**, I'm the sole developer of a self-maintaining AI research system: roughly **18,000 lines of Python**, 29 CLI tools, a 19-table datastore, and a **365-finding knowledge base**. Its current detection recall is **94%**, verified through three-judge triangulation.
- At **Cribb**, I built AI-assisted listing comparison and extraction, a document-verification pipeline with AWS Textract, and a remote MCP server that lets agents propose actions while keeping consequential decisions with a person.
- At **AudiPsalm**, I reduced average query time by **40%** across three production databases and helped cut releases from **45 minutes to under five**.
- As a **freelance developer**, I work directly with non-technical clients to turn an idea into a shipped website, payment flow, or automation—and support it after launch.
